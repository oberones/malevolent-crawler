# Malevolent Gods: The Drowned Labyrinth

A browser roguelite dungeon crawler set in an original world of coastal cosmic horror.

At Veyr Quay, each receding tide reveals another stair into the Drowned Observatory.
A bell beneath its foundations sounds only inside the listener. The things that
answer remember streets the town has never built.

You are a relic seeker. Descend through impossible chambers, confront the presences
below the breakwater, and bring back relics that will outlast your next expedition.

## Explore the Drowned Observatory

Each descent unfolds through randomized encounters and discoveries. Follow
tide-marked doors, open salvage coffers, and decide which disturbances to leave
undisturbed. Pause your exploration to rest, or press on toward the Threshold
Keepers guarding the way deeper. Sealed chambers conceal the more powerful Deep
Presences.

The observatory's inhabitants range from quay scavengers and warped shore creatures
to the Choir in the Wall and the Lantern Without Flame. Creature portraits, relic
illustrations, music, and sound effects give the depths their unsettling character.

## Shape your relic seeker

- **Prepare for each expedition.** Distribute 20 stat points and choose a passive
  skill before descending. Build around damage, resilience, attack speed, critical
  hits, or healing through vampirism.
- **Let your build fight.** Attacks happen automatically once combat begins. Your
  stats, equipment, and passive skill determine how the battle unfolds; attempts to
  flee an encounter can fail.
- **Grow in Attunement.** Each upgrade offers three bonus-stat choices and two
  rerolls, letting you adapt your build during a descent.
- **Collect recovered relics.** Equip up to six pieces drawn from 14 weapon, armor,
  shield, and helmet categories. Discover Tideglass Edges, Tolling Mauls, Diving
  Reliquaries, and more across six rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary,
  and Heirloom. Sell unwanted inventory relics for Quay Marks.
- **Make offerings to the depths.** Spend Quay Marks on Tide Offerings for random
  bonuses that last through the run. Deepening Curses strengthen enemies while
  improving loot quality.

## Return to the quay and descend again

Death or abandoning an expedition ends the current run. Attunement, run bonuses,
skills, stat allocation, and observatory progress reset, giving you another chance
to try a different build.

Your recovered relics, inventory, Quay Marks, name, and lifetime statistics remain.
Each expedition can leave you better equipped for the next journey below.

## Save your expedition

Progress saves locally in your browser. Returning in the same browser at the same
site lets you continue your current descent, including an unfinished encounter.
The Expedition Journal provides statistics, settings, and character export and
import controls.

Export your character to keep a portable copy of your holdings or move to another
browser. Importing replaces the current character after confirmation and starts a
fresh run; it does not transfer the active descent. Export before clearing browser
data, and keep one gameplay tab active at a time. If the game reports an unsaved
session, keep it open and use the recovery or export options before leaving.

For local setup, self-hosting, or contributing, see the
[contributor and agent guide](AGENTS.md).
